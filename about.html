⚰️ About Lotto Graveyard

Welcome to Lotto Graveyard, where your lottery dreams come to rest. This mystical application analyzes your Powerball numbers against years of historical data and reveals their fate through the ancient art of tarot.

What We Do

Lotto Graveyard provides a unique blend of data analysis and mystical insight:

  • Historical Analysis: Compare your numbers against 1,497+ Powerball drawings dating back to January 2015
  • Near-Miss Detection: See how close your numbers have come to winning
  • Frequency Tracking: Discover how often your numbers appear in the past year
  • Mystical Tombstone: View your numbers' fate displayed on a gothic tombstone
  • Tarot Reading: Receive a 3-card reading (Past, Present, Future) based on your numbers

How It Works

Enter your 5 white ball numbers (1-69) and your Powerball number (1-26), or scan your ticket with your device's camera. We'll analyze your numbers and display their historical performance on a mystical tombstone.

Your lottery numbers are then used to deterministically select three tarot cards from the 1909 Rider-Waite deck. The same numbers always produce the same cards, connecting your fortune to the numbers you've chosen.

Technology

Lotto Graveyard is built with:

  • Pure JavaScript (no frameworks) for fast, lightweight performance
  • OCR technology for ticket scanning (Tesseract.js)
  • Client-side processing for privacy (no data sent to servers)
  • 1909 Rider-Waite tarot imagery for authentic mystical aesthetic
  • Responsive design that works on mobile and desktop

Your Privacy

We don't collect, store, or transmit your lottery numbers. All analysis happens right in your browser. Camera access is only used for scanning tickets and is released immediately after. See our Privacy Policy for details.
