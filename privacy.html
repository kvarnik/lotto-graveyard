🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Overview

Lotto Graveyard is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect (spoiler: very little) and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Information You DON'T Provide

We do not collect or store your lottery numbers. All number analysis happens entirely in your web browser. Your numbers never leave your device.

Camera Access

If you choose to scan a lottery ticket:

  • We request access to your device's camera
  • Camera access is only used for scanning ticket numbers
  • No images or video are stored or transmitted
  • Camera access is released immediately after scanning
  • You can always use manual entry instead

Browser Storage

We use browser localStorage to cache historical lottery data for faster performance. This data contains:

  • Public Powerball drawing results (dates and winning numbers)
  • No personal information
  • Stored locally on your device only
  • Can be cleared by clearing your browser data

How We Use Information

All processing happens in your browser:

  • Number Analysis: Compares your numbers to historical data locally
  • Tarot Selection: Generates cards based on your numbers locally
  • Display: Shows results on your screen only

Third-Party Services

Lotto Graveyard uses the following third-party services:

  • Tesseract.js: OCR library loaded from CDN for ticket scanning (runs in your browser)
  • Seedrandom.js: Random number library for deterministic card selection (runs in your browser)

No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

Data Retention

Since we don't collect your data, there's nothing to retain. Historical lottery data cached in your browser remains until you clear your browser storage.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Use the application without providing any personal information
  • Deny camera access and use manual entry instead
  • Clear browser storage at any time
  • Use the application anonymously

Security

Since all processing happens in your browser and no data is transmitted, your information is as secure as your device. We recommend:

  • Using a modern, updated web browser
  • Not sharing screenshots containing your lottery numbers publicly
  • Closing the browser tab when finished

Children's Privacy

Lotto Graveyard is intended for entertainment purposes only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Check the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Lotto Graveyard after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us via the repository where this code is hosted.

Disclaimer

Lotto Graveyard is for entertainment purposes only. We are not affiliated with any lottery organization. Historical lottery data is provided as-is for analysis purposes. Tarot readings are for entertainment and not meant as financial advice.
